This local subset contains Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh,
Chhattisgarh, and Telangana. Geometries are from the pinned simplified
source without hand-drawn changes. English labels and representative label points
were derived by scripts/prepare_ui_boundaries.py. Boundaries are display-only and
do not change the forecast grid or the model's predictions.

V2: india-states.geojson contains all 36 state/UT ADM1 geometries from the
same pinned, checksum-verified source. Only the original six central-India
map labels remain visible to avoid overcrowding the approved map design.
The 2011 ADM1 source is geographic context, not a claim about current legal
boundaries. State inference uses 0.5-degree grid centers inside each polygon;
five small state/UT units have no such center and receive no fabricated result.
